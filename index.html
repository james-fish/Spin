Getting it into your gear

Octatrack: share the take, choose "Save to Files", then copy it to the OT's card (AUDIO folder) in Files with a USB-C card reader.

Loopy Pro: share and pick Loopy Pro if it shows up in the share sheet. If not, save to Files and import into a clip, or copy the file in Files and paste it into Loopy.

Tonverk, live: connect the phone to the Tonverk over USB-C, sample its USB input and spin. That route needs no files at all.

Safari can't send audio straight into AUM. iOS only routes audio between apps through AUv3 or inter-app audio, and a web page can't use either.
