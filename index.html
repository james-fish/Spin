GETTING IT INTO YOUR GEAR

Octatrack: share the take and choose "Save to Files" (the citation .txt comes along), then copy the WAV to the OT card's AUDIO folder with a USB-C card reader.

Loopy Pro: share and pick Loopy Pro if it appears; otherwise save to Files and import into a clip.

Tonverk, live: connect the phone over USB-C and sample its USB input. Needed for YouTube and any station marked "play only".

Safari can't send audio straight into AUM: iOS only routes audio between apps through AUv3 or inter-app audio. Map and timeline show what the source provides; many archive files have a date but no place, and approximate local time for live streams is worked out from longitude.
